Andrew & Kate: Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Andrew & Kate does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • It makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • It reads the phone's motion sensors only to move the puzzle pieces on screen. This data stays on the device and is not recorded.
  • Anything the app remembers between launches is kept only on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
